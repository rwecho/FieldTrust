PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy

Last updated: September 16, 2026.

This notice covers the FieldTrust marketing website and customer-discovery form. FieldTrust is currently in product development. It does not yet describe the privacy practices of a released mobile application.

Information you choose to provide

If you use the customer-discovery form, you may provide your name, work email, company, role, team size, industry, current photo workflow, pain points, requested features, and additional workflow context.

Why we collect it

We use submitted information to understand field-work workflows, validate product requirements, respond to product inquiries, invite suitable users to research or early-access programs, and improve FieldTrust.

Form delivery

The website currently uses FormSubmit to deliver form responses to rwecho@live.com. This means a third-party service may process the form submission in order to deliver it. Please do not submit passwords, financial information, health information, government identifiers, trade secrets, or other sensitive/confidential information through this form.

Retention and deletion

We may keep customer-discovery submissions for as long as they remain useful for product research or follow-up. You can ask us to delete information you submitted by emailing rwecho@live.com.

Public project examples

The website uses public U.S. project names and generated product screenshots only as realistic interface examples. They do not indicate endorsement, partnership, deployment, or affiliation with the referenced owners, agencies, contractors, or projects.

Future app privacy

Before a public mobile release, we will publish a separate application privacy policy covering account data, photos, location data, cloud synchronization, analytics, retention, and deletion controls.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: rwecho@live.com.
