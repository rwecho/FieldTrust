FEATURES

Everything your field team needs to capture, organize, and prove work.

FieldTrust focuses on the field-photo workflow your team repeats every day—not the complexity of a full construction ERP.

⌖ GPS Confidence

See location accuracy before every shot. Weak indoor positioning is clearly flagged.

◫ Ghost Overlay

Align an After photo with the original Before image for trustworthy comparisons.

☁ Local-first Capture

Every photo is safely persisted on the device before cloud sync begins.

✓ Verified Reports

Generate reports with a verification badge, report ID, and scan-to-verify entry point.

◉ Voice Notes

Capture context hands-free while work is happening instead of typing on site.

▥ Project Timeline

Keep Before, After, Progress, Issue, and completion photos attached to the right job.
