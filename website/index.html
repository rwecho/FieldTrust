THE PRODUCT

Field documentation without the field-office friction.

FieldTrust is built around the work your team repeats every day: open the right project, capture reliable evidence, add context, and hand over a clear record.

01

GPS Confidence

See the location source and accuracy before every photo. Weak indoor or assisted positioning is visible instead of silently becoming false precision.

02

Ghost Overlay

When shooting an After photo, overlay the original Before image so crews can match the angle and make comparisons credible.

03

Local-first Capture

A photo is considered captured only after it is safely persisted on the device. Uploading never blocks field work.

04

Verified Reports

Turn project photos and notes into a clean report with a verification badge, report ID, and integrity check entry point.

USE CASES

See exactly where FieldTrust fits into the job.

Different trades have different workflows, but the evidence problem is the same: prove what was there, what changed, who captured it, and what the customer received.

GENERAL CONTRACTOR · CONSTRUCTION

Progress documentation & owner handover

A superintendent walks the site every afternoon. Instead of dumping hundreds of photos into a camera roll, every capture lands in the correct project with time, location confidence, stage, and note.

Capture progress→Flag issues→Before / After→Handover report
OutcomeA searchable record for owners, PMs, warranty questions, and dispute prevention.
HVAC · FIELD SERVICE

Service-call proof before leaving site

A technician documents the unit condition, serial plate, replaced part, completed repair, and final operating state while still at the customer location.

  • GPS-backed arrival context
  • Voice note for work performed
  • Before / After repair photos
  • Customer-ready service report
PROPERTY · INSPECTION

Move-in, move-out & condition records

An inspector walks every room and exterior area with a consistent capture sequence. Damage stays tied to the right property, date, area, and inspection stage.

  • Room-by-room evidence
  • Issues with photo context
  • Repeatable comparison angles
  • Shareable condition report
INFRASTRUCTURE · CIVIL

Document changing field conditions at scale

Road, transit, utility, and civil teams need a record that survives crew changes and long project timelines. FieldTrust keeps progress photos attached to project, place, stage, and capture metadata.
